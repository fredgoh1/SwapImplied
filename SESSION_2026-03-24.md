# Session Notes — 2026-03-24

## Objective
Build a historical time series of USD/SGD FX swap implied SGD interest rates (1M, 3M, 6M)
from 1 January 2025, and produce moving average breakout charts to assess whether recent
data points represent a rate breakout.

---

## Data Sourced

| File | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| `sofermm1rt_daily_historical-data-03-24-2026.csv` | Barchart.com (SOFERMM1.RT) | 1M CME Term SOFR fixings, 2019–Mar 2026 |
| `sofermm3rt_daily_historical-data-03-24-2026.csv` | Barchart.com (SOFERMM3.RT) | 3M CME Term SOFR fixings |
| `sofermm6rt_daily_historical-data-03-24-2026.csv` | Barchart.com (SOFERMM6.RT) | 6M CME Term SOFR fixings |
| `FWD_Points_Actual_Historical.xlsx` | Bloomberg / ABS Benchmarks | USDSGD 1M/3M/6M forward points, Jan 2025–Feb 2026 |

USDSGD spot FX rates for the historical period were fetched programmatically via
**yfinance** (ticker `USDSGD=X`) — no manual download needed.

**Note on Term SOFR availability**: The official CME Term SOFR fixings are not available
free of charge via FRED or the NY Fed. The best free source found is Barchart.com
(tickers SOFERMM1.RT / SOFERMM3.RT / SOFERMM6.RT), which tracks the official CME rates
and allows one CSV download per day on a free account (covers up to 2 years back).

## Code Changes

### 1. Bug fix — `HolidayCalendar` multi-year support
**File:** `calc_swap_implied/calculate_swap_implied_rates.py`

The `HolidayCalendar` class previously hardcoded holidays for 2026 only, silently
producing wrong business day calculations for 2025 trade dates.

**Fix:** Refactored to accept `years=[2025, 2026]`. Added `_get_us_holidays_for_year(year)`
and `_get_sg_holidays_for_year(year)` helpers with hardcoded holiday dates for each year.
Holiday sets are built by iterating over all requested years. Fully backward-compatible —
`HolidayCalendar()` still defaults to 2026.

Also updated `process_excel_file()` to pass all years found in the input dataset rather
than just the first year.

**2025 US holidays added (NY SIFMA):** Jan 1, Jan 20, Feb 17, Apr 18, May 26, Jul 4,
Sep 1, Oct 13, Nov 11, Nov 27, Dec 25.

**2025 Singapore holidays added (MOM):** Jan 1, Jan 29, Jan 30, Mar 31, Apr 18, May 1,
May 12, Aug 11 (National Day observed), Oct 20, Dec 25.

### 2. New script — `build_historical_dataset.py`
Standalone backfill script at the project root. Performs end-to-end:
1. Loads Barchart SOFR CSVs (decimal format → multiplied ×100 for %)
2. Loads `FWD_Points_Actual_Historical.xlsx` (strips footer/timestamp noise)
3. Downloads USDSGD spot FX via yfinance
4. Inner-joins on Trade Date for each tenor
5. Runs `SwapImpliedRateCalculator` with `HolidayCalendar(years=[2025, 2026])`
6. Writes `output_historical_1m/3m/6m.xlsx` (same schema as output_master files)

**Output:** 267–269 rows per tenor, 2025-01-02 to 2026-02-27.

### 3. New script — `plot_ma_analysis.py`
Analysis and charting script. Performs:
1. Merges `output_historical_*.xlsx` + `output_master_*.xlsx` per tenor
   - Deduplicates on Trade_Date, preferring master-file values for the ~30-day overlap
   - Saves `output_combined_1m/3m/6m.xlsx` (281–283 rows, Jan 2025 – Mar 24 2026)
2. Computes 60-day rolling MA on `Implied_SGD_Rate_Pct` (full history, `min_periods=1`)
3. Produces **8 PNG charts**:
   - 4 × "recent" view: Oct 2, 2025 – present, y-axis auto-scaled to visible range
   - 4 × "full" view: Jan 2025 – present
   - Each set: 1 combined 3-panel PNG + 3 individual PNGs (one per tenor)
4. Prints a console breakout summary

**Interpretation:** The 3M and 6M implied rates are marginally above their 60-day MAs
(~1–2 bp), while 1M is marginally below. This is not a decisive breakout — the rates
appear to be in a gradual upward drift from lows in early 2026, but well below the
elevated levels seen in mid-2025 (~2–3%).

## Data Gaps

The `output_historical_*.xlsx` files end on 2026-02-27 (last date in
`FWD_Points_Actual_Historical.xlsx`). The `output_master_*.xlsx` files start from
2026-01-29. There may be a few missing trading days in late February / early March 2026
if the daily pipeline was not run every day.

**To backfill the gap:** extend `FWD_Points_Actual_Historical.xlsx` to cover the missing
dates and re-run `python3 build_historical_dataset.py`.
